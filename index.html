⚙️ Client Guide: How Airbnb iCal Sync Works

1. Where to find your Airbnb iCal Export URL:
In your Airbnb Host Dashboard, go to: Listings ➔ Select Listing ➔ Pricing and Availability ➔ Calendar Sync ➔ Export Calendar. Copy the .ics feed link.

2. Linking with Google Calendar:
In Google Calendar, click Other calendars + ➔ From URL and paste your Airbnb .ics link. Name it Airbnb Availability.

3. Website Live Sync:
This website periodically fetches your iCal feed URL to automatically block out reserved dates in real-time without manual updates!
